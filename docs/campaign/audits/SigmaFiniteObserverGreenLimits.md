# Sigma-finite observer Green limits

The source is sigma-finite and supported almost everywhere on the bounded open union domain; the signed source density is measurable and integrable. The observer is locally finite with the stated local upper-growth bound. Kernels are in source-observer order, `greenFunction V y x`, with the existing zero diagonal convention. No support assumption is made for individual exhaustion domains.

For an increasing open exhaustion, the module proves product integrability, product L¹ convergence, observer-almost-everywhere convergence, observer L¹ convergence and a uniform operator bound; it also treats densities varying in source L¹. For shrinking distance cutoffs, it proves product integrability and L¹ convergence, slice integrability and observer-almost-everywhere convergence, observer L¹ convergence, and varying-density observer L¹ convergence. The proofs use the existing sigma-finite weighted-product theorem, Green monotonicity and exhaustion, dominated convergence, and the uniform observer bound. The potential-theoretic infrastructure uses work of Leonardo Bonanno.
