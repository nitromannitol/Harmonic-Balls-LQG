# Source-free transposed Green potentials of integrable densities

This is deterministic infrastructure, not closure of a numbered result. For a
bounded open planar domain, a finite measure almost everywhere carried by the
domain, and a measurable real integrable density vanishing almost everywhere
on an open subset, the theorem proves integrability of the literal weighted
kernel at every observer in that subset and harmonicity of its integral there.
The kernel order is `greenFunction V y x`.

The positive and negative density measures are finite and vanish on the open
subset, even when the original measure does not. The uniform off-source bound
on a singleton observer gives integrability for each density measure. The
with-density integral formulas identify the literal weighted integral with the
difference of their potentials at every observer in the subset. Finite-source
harmonicity and local equality then establish the result. No reciprocity or new
literature input is used. The potential-theoretic infrastructure uses work of
Leonardo Bonanno. The printed axioms are `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and
`Quot.sound`.
