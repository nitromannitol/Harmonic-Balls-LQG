## Finding and exact scope

The requested zero–one law is already proved: `IsWholePlaneGFF.origin_germ_trivial` in `HarmonicBalls/Proofs/Sec8/OriginGermZeroOne.lean:82`. For any measurable space `Ω`, measure `P`, `hC`, `hcirc`, `h : IsWholePlaneGFF P hC hcirc`, and real `α₀`, it proves

```lean
  MeasurableSet[Section6.fieldSigmaAlgebraModConst α₀ hC (Metric.ball 0 ε)] A) →
  P A = 0 ∨ P A = 1
```

The 2026-11-06 update in `~/paper-fleets/hb/INPUT-REQUESTS.md:269` supersedes its earlier request. Its candidate at lines 38–48 uses mean-zero pairings without the deterministic log shift; `Section6.fieldSigmaAlgebraModConst_eq` (`Proofs/Sec6/AnnulusIteration.lean:22`) and `fieldPairing` at `α₀ = 0` identify that candidate with the theorem. The project sigma-algebra is generated by the individual mean-zero smooth compactly supported test pairings, `Draft/Section6.lean:143–151`; it is a raw sigma-algebra, not the ambient completion. The normalized covariance and the joint Gaussian process are specified in `Defs/GFF.lean:34, 80–105`, without assuming simultaneous samplewise linearity. A deterministic `-α₀ log |·|` insertion changes only deterministic pairing values and leaves the sigma-algebra unchanged.

## Mathematical route and completion

Let `H` be the closed Gaussian first chaos of the mean-zero test pairings; `H_r` is the closed span of tests supported in `B(0,r)`. Let `K` be the closed span of pairings against `Δψ` for compactly supported smooth `ψ` vanishing near 0. The Green identity and the mean-zero condition imply that any `H_r` is orthogonal to every such probe with `ψ = 0` on `B(0,r)`. The covariance calculation is implemented in `Proofs/Sec8/ExteriorLaplacianIndependence.lean:19,57,76`; Gaussianity promotes it to independence of the generated sigma-algebras, including the monotone union over shrinking excluded balls (`ExteriorLaplacianGerm.lean:41–121`).

Moreover `K = H`: for a mean-zero test `φ`, take its planar logarithmic potential `u`, satisfying the appropriate Laplacian identity, and multiply it by a compact outer cutoff and a logarithmic inner cutoff vanishing near zero. The logarithmic inner cutoff has vanishing Dirichlet energy (a point has zero planar Dirichlet capacity); mean zero gives the decay needed at infinity. Consequently the covariance seminorm of `Δψ_k − φ` tends to zero. The exact test, cutoff, and covariance statement is `OriginGermZeroOne.lean:22–80`; the key weighted energy limit is `WeightedPuncturedCutoffEnergy.lean:115`. Thus `∩_r H_r = {0}`: a vector in the intersection is orthogonal to dense `K`. This first-chaos statement alone is not a proof of sigma-algebra triviality; use Gaussian independence and generation below.

The approximation gives convergence in probability of each test pairing and hence an almost-surely equal measurable version for the probe sigma-algebra. `completed_field_generation_of_laplacian_approximation` (`PuncturedFieldGeneration.lean:43–72`) turns this into an a.e. probe representative for *every raw full-field event*, even with an uncountable family of tests. A raw germ event is independent of the probe sigma-algebra and has such a representative, so it is independent of itself and has probability 0 or 1 (`ExteriorLaplacianGerm.lean:123–172`). The proof needs only coordinatewise Gaussian laws and covariance limits; it does not construct a common distribution-valued sample. This respects E55 (`docs/campaign/audits/UnrepresentableGFFExistence.md:3–7`).

Completion also preserves the event assertion: let `r_n = 1/(n+1)` and suppose `A` has a raw `B_n ∈ F_{r_n}` with `A = B_n` almost surely for each `n`. The countable intersection of their equality events has full probability; `C = limsup B_n = ⋂_n ⋃_{k≥n} B_k` is in every `F_{r_n}` by monotonicity, and `A = C` almost surely. Cofinality yields membership in every positive-radius raw sigma-algebra, so the existing theorem applies. This uses radius-dependent representatives, not a simultaneous field representation. If the ambient space is not complete, the relevant formulation is a.e. equivalence to raw events, with `A` ambient measurable when evaluating it.

## Application to the rooted step

`PinchRootedData` (`Proofs/Sec11/ClosureScales/ManyPinchFromRootedData.lean:40–83`) chooses an arbitrary probability law `Q` and an arbitrary family `germ`; its `htrivial` quantifies over the *same set* `A` measurable in every `germ ε`. The theorem discharges that field only after constructing, on the same `Ω` under `Q`, a suitable `IsWholePlaneGFF Q hC hcirc` and choosing `germ ε` below the corresponding modulo-constant field sigma-algebra (or proving an a.e. representative bridge for it). A local absolute-continuity alternative is already proved as `origin_modConst_germ_trivial_of_local_absoluteContinuous` (`Proofs/Sec11/PinchModConstGerm.lean:41–69`); it requires the stated trimmed-measure comparison on a positive-radius full-field sigma-algebra. Neither the existence of `Q`, the locality of the pinch indicators, nor this comparison follows merely from the origin theorem. `PinchRootedDataStep` remains open; do not replace it with this lemma or stage another rooted-law input.

## Sources and distinctions

Bou-Rabee–Gwynne, [arXiv:2208.11795, Lemma 8.3 proof, p. 41](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.11795), lines 3218–3235 in the PDF extraction and corrected TeX `paper/lqg_harmonic_ball-corrected.tex:2196`, assert small-ball germ triviality and cite an analogue. Duplantier–Miller–Sheffield, [arXiv:1409.7055, Lemma 7.2, p. 126](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.7055), prove the boundary-point germ for a free-boundary GFF on the half-plane via the Markov property and a Green/Poisson-kernel calculation; it is not an exact whole-plane theorem. The present argument is the explicit whole-plane modulo-constant route, supported by the repository proofs, not an attribution of that extension to DMS.

This is a shrinking-ball germ at 0, not a tail at spatial infinity. Unit-circle normalization fixes an additive representative but does not make raw fixed-coordinate germs identical to modulo-constant germs. Fixed-test almost-sure equalities need not hold simultaneously over all tests: transporting the raw generated sigma-algebra between versions therefore requires an explicit event-level argument. Stationarity of scale observables alone does not imply ergodicity or germ triviality. Leonardo Bonanno's LatticeProb construction and ergodic formalization are credited in the existing Section 8 proof imports.
