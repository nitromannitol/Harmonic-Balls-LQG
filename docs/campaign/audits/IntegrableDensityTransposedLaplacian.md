# Integrable density transposed Green potentials

This is deterministic infrastructure, not closure of a numbered result. For a
bounded open planar domain and a finite measure almost everywhere carried by the
domain, the source density is real, measurable, and integrable. The Green
kernel retains the order `greenFunction V y x`, and the observer measure is
volume restricted to the domain.

The positive and negative density measures are finite by integrability. The
finite-source product theorem, product with-density formula, and positive-minus-
negative decomposition give weighted product integrability. On a common
full-volume event the weighted source slice is integrable and its literal
integral equals the difference of the positive and negative source potentials.
Their domain integrability gives integrability of the weighted potential, and
almost-everywhere transport gives its distributional Laplacian with negative
source first and positive source second. No reciprocity or new literature input
is used. The potential-theoretic infrastructure uses work of Leonardo Bonanno.
The printed axioms are `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
