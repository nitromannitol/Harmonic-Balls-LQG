## Exact Lean clauses and a counterexample to their combination

Write `Bₙ = weightedLocalization w n`, `Kₙ = unnormalizedWeightedRestrictionKernel ... Bₙ ... w`, `Qₙ,z = P.map (fun ω => shift n (z,ω))`, and `C_z(m) = m.map (fun v => v-z)`. The original has `P ≪ S`, `AEMeasurable μ S`, local finiteness S-a.e.; measurable positive finite w; `ρ n ≪ volume`; jointly measurable `shift n : ℂ × Ω → Ω`; and:

```
hroot : P ⊗ₘ Kₙ ≪ shiftedRootReferenceLaw (ρ n) P (shift n)
hac   : ∀ᵐ z ∂ρ n, Qₙ,z ≪ R n z
hdom  : ∀ᵐ z ∂ρ n, R n z ≪ S
hraw  : ∀ᵐ z ∂ρ n, (R n z).map (fun ω => C_z (μ ω)) ≪ P.map ν
```

The ambient `S` can be chosen as `P` for its initial three requirements alone: the GFF gives `AEMeasurable μ P` and `IsLQGLimit` gives local finiteness P-a.e. This does **not** solve hdom, because the desired shifted/rooted laws can be singular to P. If a suitable `S` existed, `hac` and `hdom` would imply `Qₙ,z ≪ S` for ρ-a.e. z; choosing `R n z := Qₙ,z` would make hac reflexive and preserve both hdom and hraw by transitivity of pushforward domination. Thus R adds no essential freedom once these clauses hold, and no independence or common dominating S follows from DMS.

There is a concrete obstruction to the four clauses together, even on a standard canonical GFF space. Take `γ = 1`, `α₀ = 1` (so `α₀ < Q = 5/2`), and choose the *same* LQG measure for μ and ν, constructed from `h = h^C - log|·|`. For any fixed z ≠ 0, the recentered measure `C_z μ` retains the logarithmic singularity at `-z`. Under `P.map ν` the point `-z` is away from the singularity at 0, so the field is locally an ordinary GFF plus a smooth function there.

The distinction is measurable from the measure: on each rational disc avoiding the singularity, the local field is determined by its restricted LQG measure, using [Berestycki–Sheffield–Sun, Theorem 1.1 and Remark 1.2, PDF pp. 2–3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.5407v4#page=2); the proof on PDF p. 11 gives the same local reconstruction by mollifying the measure. Apply this on a countable family of discs in the punctured plane and recover circle averages from the reconstructed distribution. For the recentered field, its circle average around `-z` divided by `log(1/r)` tends to 1; for ν around the same fixed nonzero point it tends to 0. This is the Brownian strong law for GFF circle averages plus the deterministic logarithm. Countable radii and discs make the separating event measurable; applying the same reconstruction on punctured discs to smooth logarithmic shifts is justified locally by the stated theorem and local absolute continuity. Consequently, for each z ≠ 0 there is a measurable set `A_z` of measures with `P.map ν A_z = 0` but `P[C_z μ ∈ A_z] = 1`. The sets can be chosen jointly measurable in `(z,m)` using a countable local reconstruction and translations.

Now hraw and hac imply `Qₙ,z {ω | C_z μ ω ∈ A_z} = 0` for ρ-a.e. z. Joint measurability, Fubini, and the definition of `shiftedRootReferenceLaw` give zero mass to the corresponding `(ω,z)` event under the reference law; hroot gives zero mass under `P ⊗ₘ Kₙ`. On the other hand, by the P-a.s. singularity property, non-atomicity, positive full support and local finiteness, the union of the `Bₙ` covers the plane, w is positive and finite, and at least one unnormalized weighted rooted law assigns positive mass to that event with z ≠ 0. This is a contradiction. The standard LQG facts are stated in [Bou-Rabee–Gwynne Fact 2.1, PDF p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.11795#page=9); the counterexample is a mathematical source-level argument, not a Lean-checked theorem. It does not depend on hdom. In particular hraw is too global for the locally smooth shift in the paper; it sees the pre-existing singularity. This does not refute the paper's local statement, the candidate DMS observed law, or the conditional Lean theorem.

The carrier mismatch is separate: DMS identifies a law on fields modulo constants, whereas hroot asks domination on *all* measurable subsets of the original Ω × ℂ, including unrelated coordinates. The candidate offers no `Measurable (shift n)` with target Ω, nor any lifting of a modulo-constants insertion through the field observation. Even for `α₀ = 0` and `w = 1`, its unit-disc observed equality is not hroot. For general α₀ the suggested weight agrees with `|z|^(α₀γ)` away from 0, but must be adjusted at 0 to meet positivity/finiteness and the resulting weighted localization `Bₙ` is not necessarily the unit disc. A further Weyl-scaling, restriction and σ-finite localization argument is needed to identify ρ; arbitrary `ρ n ≪ volume` cannot be inferred from the unit-disc formula alone.
