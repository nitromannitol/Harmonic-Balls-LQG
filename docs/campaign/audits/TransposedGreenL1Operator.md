# Transposed Green operator on L¹

This is deterministic infrastructure and does not close a numbered result. For
positive R, one nonnegative constant works for every open planar V contained in
the ball of radius R and every finite source measure carried by V almost
everywhere. The unique continuous real-linear operator on L¹ is characterized
by the integral of `f y * greenFunction V y x` for almost every observer x.
The characterization explicitly includes every representative equal to the L¹
class almost everywhere, with no measurability or boundedness assumption on that
representative. The operator norm is bounded by the common constant.

The mass bound is applied to the measure with density `|f|`; mixed
integrability supplies almost-everywhere slice integrability for additivity.
Source equivalence gives pointwise equality of the integral for every observer,
and hence representative independence. No reciprocity or literature input is
introduced. The potential-theoretic infrastructure uses work of Leonardo
Bonanno. Printed axioms: `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.
