# Local cone mass representation

`ConeMassLocalRepresentation.lean` proves locality under equality of restricted
measures, measurability in the measure, and invariance under normalization by
finite positive mass. Its Green-potential measurability uses
`PinchGreenMeasurable.measurable_annulusGreenPotentialSup`: this uses the
lower semicontinuity of the extended Green kernel, including its infinite
diagonal, and a countable dense supremum with proved hypotheses.

`exists_coneMassEvent_countable_encoding` derives a measurable event `C`
from the real masses on a generating pi-system. The proof makes the
measure-valued map measurable in the sigma-algebra generated by those
coordinates, and takes the preimage of the actual cone event. It does not
assume an event representation. `exists_common_continuity_ball` chooses a
radius in any prescribed nonempty interval; `exists_common_buffered_continuity_system`
constructs a common countable continuity pi-system and dyadically buffered
exhaustion for two locally finite random measures on that ball.

`IsWholePlaneGFF.aemeasurable_fixedShift_lqgMeasure` proves measurability of
the shifted actual limit via continuous Weyl scaling and uniqueness.
`IsWholePlaneGFF.exists_enclosing_fixedShift_coneMassEvent_representation`
combines all of the above with the common fixed-shift scalar decoder. For
any deterministic insertion it supplies the two required local pairing event
representations, on a common continuity ball surrounding both annuli. The
shifted reference mass is positive by equivalence of the original and Weyl
weighted measures; both reference masses are finite by local finiteness.

Remaining h5 input is the original measure's full support, exactly
`∀ᵐ ω ∂P, ∀ U : Set ℂ, IsOpen U → U.Nonempty → 0 < μ ω U`, plus its geometric
annulus inclusions and measurability, both actual LQG limits, and a shift smooth
on the larger ball. Existing `Section6.ae_fullSupport_all_singular_limits`
derives full support from `WholePlaneScaledBallMassStep` and staged
`LQGMeasureExistence`; any use of those inputs must be audited at assembly.
No positivity of the cone event or numbered closure is claimed. The printed
axioms for all declarations are `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
