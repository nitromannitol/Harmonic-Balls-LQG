# Integrable-density observer domain exhaustion

For a locally finite observer satisfying the checked local upper ball-growth condition, a bounded open domain `U`, and increasing open sets exhausting `U`, this module proves almost-everywhere and integral-norm convergence of literal source-observer Green potentials. The finite source is supported almost everywhere in `U`; the signed density is measurable and source-integrable. The observer measure is restricted to all of `U`, and no source support assumption on the individual exhaustion sets is made.

The mixed kernel is dominated by the weighted Green kernel on `U`. Domain convergence off the diagonal, the zero diagonal convention, and dominated convergence give the fixed-density results. A uniform subdomain estimate follows by comparison with the `U` kernel and the existing observer bound. The varying-density theorem combines this estimate with the fixed-density convergence and source L¹ convergence. The Green kernel retains source-observer orientation.

This is analytic infrastructure, not closure of a numbered paper result. The potential-theoretic infrastructure uses work of Leonardo Bonanno.
