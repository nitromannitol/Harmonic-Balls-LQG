One event combines a.e.-time non-Lipschitzness and all-positive-time Jordan boundaries of components of the complement of its closure. The metric clause separately quantifies ξ and D, requires `IsLQGMetricOfField` for that D, then takes an a.s. event and a.e. positive times, uniformly over every metric-ball centre w and radius s. The quantifier order permits different null sets for different D; no measurability of the family is assumed.
* 1.3 fixes x, a random family, t ≥ 0, and deterministic open U; the containment event has an a.e.-equal representative in the **uncompleted** `fieldSigmaAlgebra`. The t=0 event uses `{x}`, an explicit operator reading (E20/E21); positive times use the closure of the given family. A representative can depend on the fixed parameters. This is weaker on its face than literal measurability of the event in TeX 179–180; the arbitrary family can be modified on P-null sets, and source/completion equivalence needs a source-level decision.
