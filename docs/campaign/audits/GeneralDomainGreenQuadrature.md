It also assumes local finiteness, positive time, an open domain contained in a positive-radius disc satisfying `VolumeGrowth`, the pole in the domain, and compact containment of the cluster. No new literature input is introduced and no numbered-result closure is claimed. The frontier-null hypothesis remains to be established independently for applications.
