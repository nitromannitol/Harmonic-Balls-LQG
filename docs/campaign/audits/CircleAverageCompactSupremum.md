# Compact circle-average suprema

For any nonempty compact `K ⊆ ℂ`, `exists_measurable_compact_supremum` constructs a real-valued measurable function equal almost surely to the supremum of the actual field on `K`, assuming measurable fixed coordinates and almost-sure spatial continuity. A deterministic countable dense subset of `K` is selected before considering samples. Its real supremum is measurable for every sample, including those where the coordinates are unbounded. On the single almost-sure continuity event, compactness supplies a finite bound and continuity extends the dense-set bound to all of `K`.

`IsWholePlaneGFF.exists_measurable_fieldCircleAverage_supremum` applies this to `closedBall a R` for `R ≥ 0` and every fixed `L > 0`, including the singleton case `R = 0`. The field is `fieldCircleAverage α (hcirc ω) L`; its coordinates are measurable by the frozen GFF interface and its spatial continuity follows from `ae_continuous_fieldCircleAverage`. The latter derives continuity of the logarithmic correction and uses the interface's almost-sure continuity of the actual `hcirc`, not an unverified simultaneous equality of a separate version. This includes the whole-plane circle field by taking `α = 0`.

`IsWholePlaneGFF.exists_measurable_exp_mul_fieldCircleAverage_supremum` proves that the supremum of `exp (ξ * fieldCircleAverage ...)` has a measurable representative that is strictly positive at every sample, for any real `ξ`. On the continuity event, the real supremum is attained on the compact ball, which identifies the exponential of the chosen representative with the supremum of the exponential field. The module also provides the exponential of the unscaled field supremum. No moment or tail estimate is claimed.

The import closure contains Leonardo Bonanno's GFF and circle-average code. All four printed axiom lists contain only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`; the Lean file contains no forbidden proof tokens or heartbeat overrides.
