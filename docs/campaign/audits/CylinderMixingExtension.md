# Cylinder mixing extension: progress audit

The exact conditional target is elaborated as `cylinderMixingExtensionStatement`.
The finite-dimensional premise quantifies over both finite coordinate lists and
all bounded continuous functions on their real vector spaces. The event
conclusion uses the coordinate-generated sigma-algebra, while probability and
measure preservation refer to the original ambient measurable space.

Proved: the generated sigma-algebra is the pullback of the product
sigma-algebra and is generated by pullbacks of measurable finite cylinders.
For finite measures, every event in this sigma-algebra is approximable in
measure by such a pulled-back cylinder; the index type need not be countable.
The approximation uses Mathlib's set-ring approximation theorem, including
its finite-measure and covering hypotheses.

Still open: extend the continuous product-expectation limit to indicators of
finite-dimensional Borel sets using bounded-continuous approximation, then use
measure preservation and the cylinder approximation to extend to all events.
The statement itself remains unproved. No GFF shift invariance, law, or
sigma-algebra generation claim is made here.
