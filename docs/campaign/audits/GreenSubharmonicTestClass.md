# Green and subharmonic test-class semantics

Verdict: **bounded-representative equivalence remains unproved**. The Lean audit proves an exact reduction conditional on a precisely stated representative property, and proves the property for the harmonic-only tests. It gives no counterexample to the representative property or to a frozen numbered result. In particular, a discrepancy between two docstrings is not such a counterexample. The corrected paper introduces Brownian Green functions for bounded open O (TeX 468–469), but quantifies over open O containing a neighbourhood of the closed cluster in the test class (TeX 741–751); Lemma 4.3 and its proof are at 755–789, and the negative Green tests for Lemma 9.1 at 2285–2308. No source semantics for an arbitrary non-Greenian O is supplied by this audit.

## Frozen carriers and their actual use

* `subharmonicBallTestClass` (Draft/Section4.lean:65–69): a witness consists of open O containing `closure Λ`; compact K contained in O; finite measures ν₁, ν₂ concentrated on K; `ν₁ Λ = 0`; q harmonic on O; `ν₂ {z} = 0`; `Integrable (greenFunction O z) ν₂`; and the global function equality `f = q + greenPotential O ν₁ - greenPotential O ν₂`. Neither O nor Λ is assumed bounded in this definition. The measures are not required to be mutually singular. The support condition is concentration outside K, not an equality of topological supports. `IsSubharmonicBall` additionally requires open Λ, `z ∈ Λ`, positive finite μ-mass, and the sub-mean inequality **and** `IntegrableOn f Λ μ` for every such f (Section4.lean:44–46,79–81).
* `greenPotentialClass O D` (Defs/HarmonicBall.lean:87–89): compact K in `O \ D`, two finite measures concentrated on K, and the global difference of their real-valued potentials; it imposes no pole integrability or non-atom condition and itself does not require O to be open or bounded. Its consumer `harmonicBallTestClass` (lines 95–97) separately requires O to be bounded and open and contain `closure Λ`, and adds a function harmonic near `closure Λ`. These are different carriers; the latter cannot simply replace the subharmonic carrier.
* The Section 4 producer `isSubharmonicBall_of_isClusterOdometer` (Steps/Sec4/ClusterSubharmonicBall.lean:185–220) destructures **every** arbitrary-O witness. It uses harmonic quadrature, `GreenFunctionIntegrableAwayFromPoleStep` for ν₁ at z, and `ClusterGreenPotentialIdentityStep` separately for ν₁ and ν₂. The latter is assembled in Proofs/Sec4/ClusterGreenIdentity.lean:35,257–258 from a harmonic quadrature step and a Green correction balayage step. These are assertions about the frozen real-valued kernels and do not by themselves establish the Brownian or extended-real interpretations or the replacement obligation below.
* Section 5 regularization (Proofs/Sec5/RegularizedSubharmonic.lean:11–35) transports the **full** class by antitonicity and equality of restricted measures; Section 5 family construction requires the full property at every positive time (Draft/Section5.lean:86–91). For comparison, Section 9 uses the bounded disc `B_R`: the negative test `-G_{B_R}(·,x)` with x ≠ z in Steps/Sec9/ObstacleComparison.lean:36–56 and Proofs/Sec9/ObstacleComparison.lean:22–29, and the positive test when x lies outside the cluster in Proofs/Sec9/SubharmonicUniqueness.lean:16–39. These particular witnesses have bounded domains. Their downstream comparisons do not show that all arbitrary-O tests have bounded representatives.

## Proved scope and the remaining type

`HarmonicBalls/Audit/GreenSubharmonicTestClass.lean` defines `boundedSubharmonicBallTestClass` by adding boundedness to the witness O and proves inclusion in the frozen class. For bounded Λ, `harmonic_mem_boundedSubharmonicBallTestClass` represents any q harmonic on an open O containing `closure Λ` by the **same global q**, with zero measures and witness `O ∩ ball 0 R`. No Green identity is used.

For arbitrary μ, Λ, z, `hasSubMeanValueProperty_congr` proves that equal values at z and equality μ-restricted-to-Λ almost everywhere preserve the entire sub-mean property, including integrability. The proved equivalence `subMeanValue_all_iff_bounded_of_representatives` requires the following explicit Lean proposition (not an asserted literature input):

```lean
∀ f ∈ subharmonicBallTestClass Λ z,
  ∃ g ∈ boundedSubharmonicBallTestClass Λ z,
    f z = g z ∧ f =ᵐ[μ.restrict Λ] g
```

A concrete stronger candidate, still unresolved and not asserted true, has exact type

```lean
∀ (μ : Measure ℂ) (Λ : Set ℂ) (z : ℂ),
  IsOpen Λ → Bornology.IsBounded Λ → z ∈ Λ →
  HasBoundedSubharmonicRepresentatives μ Λ z
```

It may be sufficient, and potentially more realistic, to prove the displayed representative property only for the relevant LQG measures and clusters with their actual volume-growth/null-set hypotheses. Alternatively an independently proved comparison of the two classes' quadrature properties could avoid producing representatives. Neither follows merely from boundedness of the cluster or from the paper's wording. A natural route chooses a bounded open O' containing `closure Λ ∪ K` inside O and absorbs the difference of the two Green potentials into a harmonic correction. The exact missing analytic content is that this construction gives a harmonic correction and preserves the frozen real values **at z and μ-a.e. on Λ**, and that the new negative kernel is integrable at z. Ordinary off-diagonal Green identities and linearity of integrals do not alone give these conclusions when one of the separate Bochner integrals is nonintegrable.

## Separate singularity checks

* **At the pole:** the frozen class explicitly assumes both `ν₂ {z} = 0` and integrability of the real kernel. The latter alone does not exclude an atom: `integrable_greenFunction_dirac_self` proves that a Dirac mass at z is integrable because the kernel has a real diagonal value. If `z ∈ Λ`, `positiveMeasure_pole_null` proves `ν₁ {z} = 0` from `ν₁ Λ = 0`, but integrability of the positive potential at z is not a field of the class; the Section 4 argument supplies a separate away-from-pole step. Identification with a finite extended-real potential still needs the relevant kernel and integrability theorems.
* **Atoms on the cluster:** `greenPotential_dirac_self` proves `greenPotential O (dirac x) x = 0` for every O, even bounded O. The paper's Green kernel is infinite on the diagonal (Proposition 2.3), so for the negative Dirac test the frozen value at x differs from the extended-real value. This does not refute the desired replacement by another frozen test. The permitted test only excludes an atom at z, not at other points of Λ; if μ charges x, this discrepancy is visible to its integral. A non-atomic μ removes the countable atomic contribution for a fixed finite ν₂, but that alone does not address the other exceptional set.
* **Nonintegrability and polar sets:** `greenPotential_eq_zero_of_not_integrable` proves that the Bochner potential is zero whenever its kernel is not integrable. Off-diagonal, `greatestHarmonicMinorant` may itself be infinite and `EReal.toReal` can erase that infinity (Defs/Green.lean:58–73). Establishing any polar exceptional set and showing that the actual LQG measure does not charge it are separate obligations; no such assertion follows from the bare types, nor does non-atomicity alone imply it. Draft/Section4.lean:58–64 describes these claims, but this audit does not certify them. The TeX writes real-valued f at 743–746 while its later negative Green test is singular, so an extended-real convention also needs a source-level interpretation.
* **Signed-measure support:** for the subharmonic class ν₁ is concentrated on K and has zero mass on Λ, whereas ν₂ may charge Λ. This enforces the intended sign for the represented difference; it does not postulate that the two measures form a Jordan decomposition. For `greenPotentialClass`, both measures are concentrated on the same compact K outside D. Any equivalence with signed Radon measures and the extended-real difference has to control cancellation and integrability separately; arbitrary differences of two Lean Bochner integrals can both evaluate to junk zero.

For a non-Greenian O, Defs/Green.lean:30–31 calls the output junk; when the minorant is `⊥`, its `toReal` is zero and the off-diagonal expression is numerically the Newtonian kernel. This is conditional on establishing that the minorant is `⊥` for the O and pole in question, and is not a Brownian Green construction or a proof of an admissible bounded replacement.
