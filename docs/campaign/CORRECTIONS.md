# Corrections in paper/lqg_harmonic_ball-corrected.tex

Surgical corrections to arXiv:2208.11795, each marked `% CORRECTION [Cn]` in the corrected draft, with the published text, the
corrected text and a one-line reason. Section 11 changes below are author-approved 2026-11-06.

- [C26] Lemma 11.8 proof: replace a potentially negative bump and universal drift comparison by a nonnegative bump and the fixed-parameter factor `1201^(-γ|α₀|)`; the former claims are not valid uniformly.
- [C27] `lemma:harmonic-comparison-chain`: point to `eq:alternate-size-2-full` instead of `eq:alternate-size-2`; the potential has variable aspect ratio.
- [C28] `lemma:mu_h-bad-and-harnack`: replace the direct origin application of the separated-annulus bound by rooted ergodicity and rooted-field transfer; separation alone does not prove rooted frequency.
- [C29] `lemma:no-exterior-cone-condition`: use a rational cone contained in the given cone instead of a containing rational cone; sector mass is monotone under inclusion.
- [C30] `lemma:no-exterior-cone-condition`: replace `A_{3r,5r}` by `A_{r,2r}` in the good-event inequality; the event controls the latter annulus.

Full audited replacement submissions are retained in `docs/campaign/S11-REPLACEMENT.md`. The pinch-event replacement is not yet integrated.
