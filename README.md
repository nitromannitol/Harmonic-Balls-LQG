# Harmonic balls in Liouville quantum gravity (Lean formalization, in progress)

Lean 4 formalization of every numbered result of A. Bou-Rabee and E. Gwynne, *Harmonic balls in Liouville quantum gravity*, arXiv:2208.11795.

## Acknowledgements

Results on the Gaussian free field, Gaussian multiplicative chaos and LQG local metrics used here (for instance Gwynne-Miller's Lemma 3.1, behind
Lemma 6.5) come from Leonardo Bonanno's [`lean-formalizations`](https://github.com/leobon12/lean-formalizations), through the copies in the
`lean-v4.35` line of [Lattice-Probability](https://github.com/nitromannitol/Lattice-Probability) (`LatticeProb/Bonanno/`), which name their
source modules.
